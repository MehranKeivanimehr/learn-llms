## Putting the pieces together

A **transformer block** packages the ideas so far: **self-attention** (words gather information from each other), **Add & Norm**, an **MLP** (each word is processed on its own — much of the model's knowledge lives here), and **Add & Norm** again. LLMs stack dozens of these blocks.

## Why "Add"?

The **residual connection** adds each sub-layer's output to its input instead of replacing it. Each block only learns a *change*, and information flows straight through deep stacks.

## Why "Norm"?

**Layer normalization** rescales each vector to mean 0 and variance 1, keeping numbers healthy through many blocks. (Many modern models normalize before each sub-layer instead; the idea is the same.)
