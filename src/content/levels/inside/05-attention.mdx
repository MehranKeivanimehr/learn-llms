## Words need context

*"The animal didn't cross the street because **it** was tired."* You know "it" means the animal — streets don't get tired. To understand "it", you looked at other words. **Attention** is how a transformer does that.

## Queries, keys and values

Every word makes a **query** ("what am I looking for?"), a **key** ("what do I contain?") and a **value** ("what do I share?"). "it" compares its query with every key using a dot product, divides by √d, and softmax turns the scores into **attention weights** that add up to 1.

## Many words at once

In a real model every word does this at the same time, and the query, key and value vectors are computed from the embeddings with learned matrices.
