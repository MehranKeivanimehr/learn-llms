## A real (tiny) language model

This level runs an actual transformer in your browser: embeddings, position signals, one attention block with an MLP and residual connections, and a final layer that scores every next character. (It skips layer norm to stay tiny.) It was trained on two public-domain nursery rhymes, so it has basically memorized them.

## The loop

Generation is the loop from level 1, for real: turn the text into tokens (here, characters), get a probability for every next token, pick one (greedy or sampled), append it, repeat.

## The context window

The model can only see the **last 16 characters**. Anything earlier is simply gone. Real models see hundreds of thousands of tokens, but the limit is the same idea.

## Your turn

Make the model write **"little star"** — without typing "star" yourself. Use `prompt "<text>"` (quotes keep spaces), `temperature <0–2>`, `reroll` and `generate`, or the controls on the canvas. Careful: "little" is also in the other rhyme.
