## Attention is order-blind

Nothing in the attention comparison says *where* a word is. Shuffle the words and the scores stay the same, so "dog bites man" and "man bites dog" look identical — yet word order tells you who did what to whom.

## Adding positions

The fix is to add a **position signal** to each word's vector. The original transformer used **sinusoidal positional encodings**: each position gets a unique pattern of sine and cosine waves. Newer models use other schemes, but the idea is the same.

## Many heads

A transformer runs several attention **heads** side by side — often dozens. One might track who did an action, another might link pronouns to nouns. Their outputs are combined.
