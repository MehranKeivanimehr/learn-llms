## From probabilities to one token

Something has to **choose** one token from the model's probabilities. Always taking the most likely one (*greedy*) is predictable but dull; rolling a weighted die (*sampling*) adds variety.

## Three knobs

**Temperature** reshapes the probabilities: low makes the favourite dominate (0 = greedy), high flattens everything. **Top-k** keeps only the *k* most likely tokens. **Top-p** keeps the smallest group of top tokens whose probabilities add up to *p*.

## Why it matters

Low temperature suits facts and code; higher temperature suits brainstorming. Top-k and top-p keep some creativity while cutting off the silly long tail.
